 How to Use Anti Detection Writing Skill on Your Next Draft

Your draft can be accurate but hard to understand. Anti Detection Writing Skill lets your editor follow a repeatable editing process: understand what the reader needs, keep the facts intact, enhance flow, and review the result. You can also compare against GPT Zero.

Begin with a reply, email, or document section.

1. Install the Skill.

Run `npx skills add dexhunter/anti detection writing skill --skill anti detection writing`.

Select the editor during installation. Default installation uses current project.

2. Provide context and original text.

Tell the editor who is reading and what they need to accomplish. Include a question and any relevant docs and version limits if it's technical. Ask:

"Use anti detection writing skill to make this text smoother and easier to read for [audience]. Preserve facts and qualifications. Remove repetition and irrelevant background. Correct any facts and get separate review of final text."

The skill may adjust by moving prerequisites ahead of instructions or replacing vague pronouns. Continually check that the advice remains clear and true.

3. Measure Detector Results If Needed.

"Compare original and final text using GPT Zero Basic Scan with same display model and record exact inputs, all three scores, dates, and warnings. Record unchanged and worse results as well."

Live scanning requires access to your own detector and a browser tool your editor supports. The skill doesn't supply detectors. Separate reviews of correctness and readability apart from score.
