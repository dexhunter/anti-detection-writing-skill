# Proposed next method: exact crossover between complete drafts

September 10, 2026. **Recommend a bounded crossover ablation:** generate two complete incumbent drafts, splice their unchanged prose at one independently checked matching claim boundary, and compare a selected hybrid with a selected parent. This is an unexecuted proposal. It offers an attributable output intervention; no detector gain is predicted or established.

The repository inspected was `https://github.com/dexhunter/anti-detection-writing-skill/tree/4c3a7edc3683d1a59dbfc0f0658339a1d1785887` at `4c3a7edc3683d1a59dbfc0f0658339a1d1785887`, with a clean reported working tree. Research used the public study READMEs, GEPA's `sources/development-history.json`, exposed method policies, and `references/detector-research.md`. No private reports or confirmation cases were opened. No target drafts, repository edits, or scans were produced.

## Why this intervention is distinct

The child is an exact prefix from one complete draft followed by an exact suffix from the other. A small deterministic assembler performs the crossover after a separate agent verifies the boundaries. It generates no new wording and does not ask a writer to “merge the best parts.” Every output span has a parent ID and byte offsets. The treatment changes which already-written spans coexist in the answer.

This is **a new constrained classical crossover variant**, not a reproduction of LMX, Mind Evolution, or GEPA. Its editorial hypothesis is that two valid complete drafts may have complementary strong passages. Its detector hypothesis remains weak: all inherited text is still AI-origin, and the available scores do not diagnose a cause that splicing would fix.

## Frozen one-case protocol

1. **Parents.** Use the reused HTTP development source packet and frozen incumbent policy. Two fresh isolated writers independently produce one complete answer each, with every required claim and protected literal and a natural ceiling of 140 prose words. Neither receives the other output, target detector history, or crossover instructions. Save exact prompts, outputs, hashes and exposed runtime metadata. No resampling or diversity-forcing instruction.
2. **Independent boundary review.** Give a fresh source-aware reviewer both complete parents and the full contract. Reject either factual failure. Enumerate only internal boundaries between complete sentences or paragraphs where both prefixes cover the same nonempty proper subset of required claims and both suffixes cover the complementary claims. A condition, negation, exception, temporal qualification, comparison, pronoun reference or explanatory attachment must not become ambiguous or change scope across the proposed seam. Claim-label equality alone is insufficient. Record exact offsets and source-supported reasons. Do not rewrite to manufacture compatibility.
3. **Deterministic assembly.** Choose one eligible boundary pair before selection or scans: minimize distance between prefix claim count and half the total claim count; break ties by ascending parent-A then parent-B byte offset. Produce both reciprocal hybrids from exact slices, allowing only one predeclared seam newline. Require each to inherit meaningful, distinct complete prose from both parents, retain all protected strings, and meet the 140-word ceiling. Preserve the assembly and span manifest. No usable seam or no actual hybrid is an honest no-op; stop without scans.
4. **Selection and final review.** Use the same frozen source-aware selection rubric in two fresh contexts: choose between the two parents for the control, and between the two hybrids for the treatment. Select on correctness, relevance, clarity and professional tone, without detector results; fix tie-breaking by candidate ID. A separate final reviewer receives only the selected exact pair, complete source evidence and claim inventory under neutral labels. Both must preserve every claim, code/command correspondence and operational qualification, with no readability loss for the hybrid. Retain rejection and stop; no repair or alternative selection after final review. Labels hide method assignments, although shared wording can reveal provenance.
5. **Optional measured pair.** Only after those gates, declare `prose_only`, freeze the selected full-message and prose hashes, then complete fresh comparable Basic scans of the selected parent and hybrid. Keep inline identifiers and explanatory prose; exclude only independently identified code blocks and standalone commands. Reserve at most 280 development words. With the coordinator's live allowance of 783 words, this leaves the planned 500-word reserve for two conditional confirmation pairs at natural ceilings of 125 words per text, plus three words. Never pad or remove claims to fit. Check actual effective editor counts before submission.

Any code blocks or standalone commands remain byte-identical immutable units, carried exactly once with their explanation. A seam that changes their attachment is ineligible. All unsuccessful parents, boundaries, hybrids, reviews and receipts remain in the denominator. Confirmation remains sealed and is considered only on a genuine reviewed paired gain under a separately frozen confirmation protocol; this proposal does not open it.

## Primary-source provenance and limitations

- **LMX paper:** [Meyerson et al., *Language Model Crossover*, revised paper §§4.3–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.12170v3#S4.SS4). Its classical text-splicing baseline operates on image prompts; the paper explicitly relies on image models tolerating ungrammatical prompt text. Its sentence experiment uses generated offspring and sentiment/embedding measures, with no exact-fact professional-prose or GPTZero result. Those results motivate distinguishing mechanisms, not expecting transfer.
- **Official implementation:** the [author-owned LMX repository](https://github.com/jal278/lmx) was pinned to `b8fec5dd16451cf7afb6e02ac621b71d5bdfee7f` (June 17, 2024). [`scripts/stablediffusion.py`, lines 61–69](https://github.com/jal278/lmx/blob/b8fec5dd16451cf7afb6e02ac621b71d5bdfee7f/scripts/stablediffusion.py#L61-L69) implements ordinary crossover by splitting two parents on whitespace and joining one prefix with the other suffix at a random index. It has none of our claim alignment, exact-byte inheritance, reciprocal construction or deterministic seam checks. The [sentence notebook](https://github.com/jal278/lmx/blob/b8fec5dd16451cf7afb6e02ac621b71d5bdfee7f/scripts/lmx_sentiment_demo.ipynb) instead samples multiple archive parents and generates continuations. These are different operators.
- **Response-search corroboration:** [Mind Evolution, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.09891v1#S3.SS2) recombines parent solutions with evaluations and criticism, followed by refinement. It combines mechanisms on planning tasks. No author-owned implementation was located in this search, so third-party demos are not treated as official code or used here.
- **Rejected alternative:** [WRitEer, AAAI 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40232) combines editor prompt evolution with writer DPO. Omitting its training and renaming existing reflection prompts would not implement that method. It is a poorer fit for the present bounded, purchase-free experiment.

Implementation requires existing isolated agents and a small standard-library byte assembler, with no model weights, new API, token probabilities, training, or purchases. Agent usage is still a cost. Record unavailable model/settings/token data as unavailable. The independent source review remains fallible, and agents on a shared filesystem have operational rather than security-enforced isolation unless the coordinator supplies a stronger boundary. A short answer may offer no compatible seam; the honest outcome then is non-activation. A single reused-case result would establish only the observed crossover comparison, not generalization or a default skill improvement.
