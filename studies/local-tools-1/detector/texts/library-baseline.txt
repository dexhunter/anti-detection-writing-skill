Juniper Reach Library should extend its room-booking pilot for six weeks to test access and booking reliability before deciding whether to add rooms.

In the first eight weeks, two rooms offered free, two-hour bookings during staffed opening hours. Of 160 slots, 118 were reserved and 91 used. Reservations indicate demand without establishing reliable access. Of 42 voluntary feedback respondents, 25 valued predictable space for study or small-group work; 17 struggled to find a slot or complete a booking. These responses cannot establish how common those difficulties are among residents.

Four repeat bookers accounted for 18 of 27 unused reservations; most unreserved slots were before noon on weekdays. Cancellation habits may explain unused reservations; residents’ availability may explain low weekday-morning demand, an untested possibility. Unused reservations alone do not establish the extent of unmet demand. Adding rooms would not necessarily resolve cancellation habits or a mismatch between booking times and residents’ availability.

The online form requires an email address, while telephone bookings depend on the front-desk supervisor being available. Three of the 17 respondents reporting difficulties said one of these barriers prevented booking. During the extension, any trained desk worker should take telephone and in-person reservations using the same inventory as online bookings. Reminders and a simple cancellation option could help residents release unwanted reservations; their effectiveness is unmeasured.

The service manager should arrange training and publish weekly totals for reservations, attendance, cancellations and unfulfilled requests, broken down by booking route and time of day. Desk workers should record requests consistently, including when rooms are full.

After six weeks, expansion should be considered only if all three proposed conditions are met: trained staff can cover every staffed opening period; at least 85% of extension reservations are used or cancelled before their start time; and unfulfilled requests are recorded in at least four of the six weeks despite the changes. Meeting these conditions would support considering expansion, but would not establish how many additional rooms are needed. If staff coverage or booking reliability remains weak, revise the booking process and reassess before expansion.
