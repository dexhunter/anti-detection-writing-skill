Hawthorn Museum family map workshop (fictional museum and event)

The workshop is on 18 November, 14:00 to 15:30, in the ground-floor studio. It is intended for children aged eight to twelve, and every child must attend with an adult.

Each £6 booking includes one child and one adult. There are twelve child places. Book at the museum reception desk by 16 November; your place is confirmed only after payment.

Materials are provided. Please arrive ten minutes early through the courtyard entrance. The studio has step-free access, but no sign-language interpreter will be provided for this workshop.

If fewer than six child places have been paid for by the booking deadline, the museum will cancel the workshop and issue full refunds. Cancellation notices will go to the email address supplied at booking.

The permanent galleries will remain open that afternoon, with the usual entry charge.
