1. Lantern Trail is invented for two to four players. The first player to reach space 12 wins. The game has no dice.
2. A track with spaces numbered 1 to 12 is used; players' pawns start at space 1. Each player starts with one Lantern Token. Pawns may share a space.
3. The youngest player starts; turns proceed clockwise.
4. On each turn select a normal forward move of one space, or stay and take one Lantern Token. Maximum token holdings: three tokens; staying to take a token is not allowed if three are held. A normal one-space move cannot pass space 12 because reaching it immediately ends the game.
5. Before moving, you may instead spend two tokens to move forward three spaces. Pay before moving. A paid move that would pass space 12 leaves the pawn in place; spent tokens are not returned.
