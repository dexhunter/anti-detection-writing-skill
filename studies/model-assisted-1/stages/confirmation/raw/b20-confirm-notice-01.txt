Notices - Hawthorn Museum Family Workshop

A map workshop for children and adults is hosted at the Hawthorn Museum. Session day is 18th of November from 14:00 to 15:30 at ground studio. For this one day event, registration and booking may be completed at the museum reception desk before 16th November. Registration confirms attendance, which includes booking both for one child and one adult at cost £6.

Materials provided free of charge. Participants must arrive ten minutes early and use courtyard entrance. The ground floor studio is step free but will not offer an interpreter for deaf sign language. If fewer than six participants are registered by this booking deadline, whole refunds will cancel the session.

Notifications of cancellations will go to email address supplied during booking. Nearby permanent exhibition spaces remain open that afternoon with usual entry charges applying.
