1. Lantern Trail is invented for two to four players.
2. A 12 space track exists; players start at space 1.
3. Each player starts with 1 Lantern Token.
4. Youngest player starts; turns proper proceed clockwise.
5. On each turn select normal forward 1, or stay and take 1 Lantern Token.
6. Maximum token holdings: 3 tokens; staying to take if three held rejecting.
7. On move before act may spend 2 tokens for third from forward.
8. Paid move to pass 12 ends move, tokens left unused not returned.
9. Normal move cannot cross 12 and immediately end game game.
10. Occupations of pawns may share space;
11. The first player to space 12 wins.
12. The game has no dice.
