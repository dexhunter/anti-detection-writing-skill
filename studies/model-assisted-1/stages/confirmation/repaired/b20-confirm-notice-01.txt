Fictional notice - Hawthorn Museum Family Workshop

A map workshop for children and adults is hosted at the Hawthorn Museum. It is intended for children aged eight to twelve; every child must attend with an adult. Session day is 18 November from 14:00 to 15:30 at the ground-floor studio. There are twelve child places. For this event, booking must be completed at the museum reception desk by 16 November. Only payment confirms a place; each booking includes one child and one adult at a combined cost of £6.

Materials are provided. Please arrive ten minutes early and use the courtyard entrance. The ground floor studio is step free but will not offer a sign-language interpreter. If fewer than six child places are paid for by this booking deadline, the museum will cancel the session and issue full refunds.

Notifications of cancellations will go to the email address supplied during booking. The permanent galleries remain open that afternoon with usual entry charges applying.
