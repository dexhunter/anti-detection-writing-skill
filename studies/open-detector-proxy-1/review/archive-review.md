# Archived corpus review

The corpus passes the byte, metric and measurement-scope audit. All 19 files listed in the frozen manifest still match their hashes, all 16 inference inputs match their tracked source files exactly, and all 20 archived observations match their recorded AI, mixed and human confidences, model, mode, timestamp and warnings. The source repository head matches the freeze. The machine-readable checks are in `archive-audit.json`.

The ten comparisons contain seven ties and three reductions. Pair 01 is 100 to 93 percent AI after adding an attributed quotation; the unchanged explanatory body was verified byte for byte. Pairs 07 and 08 are 100 to 0 percent AI on two different candidate texts for the same exposed Atlas business case. All seven other comparisons remain 100 to 100. These numbers are document confidences, not percentages of AI-authored words.

The four additional observations are repeated controls, not four new inputs or comparisons. The ten pairs use 16 unique texts across three recurring topics. Pair 08 uses newly generated bytes on a previously exposed case; it is neither an exact repeat nor independent held-out validation. The selection is deliberately enriched for prior ties and reductions, so pair agreement cannot estimate general detector accuracy or performance on unseen prose. Report quotation additions, adaptive rewriting, verification, crossover and paired-example rewriting separately. Even an always-tie rule gets seven of ten historical pair labels right.

Measurement scope is preserved. The selected older quote and model-assisted inputs contain explanatory prose and inline identifiers, with no standalone code or commands found during manual inspection. They retain their legacy full-input status; they were not retroactively declared prose-only. The four newer inputs have explicit prose-only records with empty code-exclusion ranges and matching full-message/prose hashes. Answer, submitted and effective editor hashes were independently checked; terminal newlines explain the recorded representation differences. All nine rejected historical quote pairs actually contain fenced material. No code was stripped and assigned an earlier whole-message score.

One metadata field remains unverified against public evidence: `displayed_words` is hardcoded as 113 and 114 for the crossover pair. Those values match the local whitespace word counts and sum to the archived 227 credits charged, but the public per-arm result files do not record displayed word counts. Treat these two values as unverified service-display metadata unless a supporting receipt is supplied. This does not affect the verified confidences, hashes, pair directions or scope. No frozen file was changed to resolve this qualification.

The archives contain maintainer-recorded UI observations and hashes, not authenticated exports from GPTZero. This audit confirms their internal correspondence; it does not authenticate the detector service. All 16 archived bodies retain AI-origin provenance, including the two rated 100 percent human. This review reused prior research context and inspected target scores; it is an independent archival audit, not a fresh blind writing-quality review. No local detector outputs or private holdout files were inspected, no models were run, and no paid or browser scans were made.

Exactly two separately frozen historical human controls are recorded in `human-controls.json`. Darwin's 1859 first-edition Introduction contributes its first eligible complete paragraph, 157 words; this paragraph explains the publication circumstances rather than a scientific mechanism. Carroll's 1865 novel contributes its first eligible complete body paragraph, 141 words. Their selection rule was saved before source retrieval and before local output inspection. Exact downloaded bytes, including internal CRLF, punctuation and Gutenberg emphasis markers, were retained without rewriting. These familiar historical passages may have appeared in model training; they are sanity checks, not a representative human false-positive sample or evidence about modern discussion replies. They must remain a separate augmentation, without assigning GPTZero targets or changing the original frozen corpus.
