# Historical control selection rule

Frozen before retrieving the source paragraphs and before seeing any local detector outputs, on 2026-09-10.

Choose Charles Darwin, On the Origin of Species (1859 first edition), for scientific/expository prose; Lewis Carroll, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1865), for fiction. In each Project Gutenberg primary text, begin at the first chapter/introduction of the actual work after contents and find the first complete prose paragraph containing 100–160 whitespace-delimited words. Exclude headings, verse, tables, Gutenberg license/front matter and contents. Preserve the selected complete paragraph's exact downloaded text, including internal line breaks and punctuation, without rewriting or truncating. Record every preceding body paragraph's word count so selection can be reconstructed. Do not use detector results to select or replace a paragraph.

This is archival reuse/source review with prior research context, not a fresh blind quality review of the writing experiments. No local detector outputs have been inspected by this reviewer.
