# Frozen diagnostic corpus

Selection was completed before any prospective Oculus or Greyscope output. Public repository archives only were read. No private confirmation or holdout file was opened. No local model, model download, browser or detector scan was used.

Rules: prioritize explicit prose_only archived Basic Scan Model 4.9b pairs; retain exact effective editor bytes, including terminal newlines; permit legacy whole inputs only when independently inspected as entirely explanatory prose with inline identifiers and no standalone code or commands. Preserve original scope; never extract prose then attach the prior full-message score. Word counts are whitespace counts in corpus.json; service counts remain separate and unavailable values remain null. Token limits are not assessed by this curator.

Selected ten pairs: the sole eligible all-prose pair from the ten historical fixed-body quotation comparisons; model-assisted r01 and r10 technical/business ties (first two measured method attempts), r20 technical tie and business reduction, later verification business reduction; explicit prose-only current sentence-crossover and paired-editing HTTP ties. Reused controls are not independent trials. The business verification uses different final bytes on the same exposed case, not an exact repeat or an unseen validation. Repeated archived control results remain observations only. The corpus is intentionally enriched for historical reductions/ties, not a representative prevalence sample.

Mechanisms remain separate: fixed-body quotation content addition, adaptive rewriting, adaptive rewriting verification, exact sentence crossover, and paired-example rewriting. The quotation comparison changes content and length; it cannot establish a rewriting benefit. Nine historical quote pairs are rejected because exact inputs contain code/commands/tree blocks. The longer paired-example article pair was not selected to keep this small experiment focused on brief explanatory replies and known bounded input lengths.

Legacy model-assisted and quote archives did not declare prose_only in their observation records. They are labeled legacy_full_input_all_prose, not relabeled as prospectively declared prose_only. Source/prose hashes and editor hashes differ where the documented entry transform added terminal newlines; local inputs use exact archived editor bytes. All included displayed models are Basic 4.9b. Observations are maintainer-recorded UI evidence, not authenticated service exports. Hashes prove bytes, not detector authenticity.

No verified pre-LLM human negative controls were obtained within this bounded archive-only preparation. Every body retains AI-origin provenance even when GPTZero says 100% human. This is a diagnostic of historical GPTZero agreement and pair ranking, not an accuracy evaluation. No score is authorship ground truth. There are only three recurring source topics (Open WebUI parameter precedence, HTTP caching, Atlas migration), and related development texts are highly dependent.

Freeze policy: do not add/remove inputs or pairs after seeing local scores. Keep failures, out-of-limit inputs and abstentions in denominators; do not truncate and compare to whole-text target scores. Any future human controls require a separately frozen augmentation and should not overwrite this corpus.
