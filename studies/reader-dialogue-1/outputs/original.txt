Juniper Reach Library should extend its room-booking pilot for six weeks before deciding whether to add more rooms. The first eight weeks show demand, but they do not yet establish that residents can access the space reliably.

The pilot offered two rooms for free, two-hour bookings during staffed hours. Of 160 available slots, 118 were reserved and 91 were used. Among 42 feedback respondents, 25 valued a predictable place for study or small-group work, while 17 reported difficulties finding a slot or completing a booking. This voluntary feedback identifies concerns without establishing how common they are among all residents.

Two details complicate the reservation total. Eighteen of the 27 unused reservations came from four repeat bookers, and most unreserved slots fell before noon on weekdays. Missed bookings may reflect cancellation habits; low daytime use may reflect when residents can attend. More rooms would not necessarily address these possibilities.

Access needs a practical test. The online form requires an email address, and telephone bookings currently depend on the front-desk supervisor being available. Three respondents said these requirements prevented a booking. During an extension, any trained desk worker should accept telephone or in-person reservations using the same inventory as online bookings. Reminders and a simple cancellation option could help residents release slots they no longer need.

The service manager should arrange training and publish weekly reservation, attendance, cancellation and unfulfilled-request totals by booking route and time of day. Desk workers should record requests consistently, including those made when rooms are full.

An expansion decision should follow the six-week extension. The proposed conditions are that trained staff can cover every staffed opening period, at least 85% of reservations are used or cancelled before their start, and unfulfilled requests persist in at least four weeks despite the changes. These conditions would support considering expansion; they would not establish how many rooms are needed. If access coverage or booking reliability remains weak, the next step should be to revise the process and reassess.
