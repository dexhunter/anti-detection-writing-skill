How to use Anti-Detection Writing Skill on your next draft

An AI draft can be accurate and still take too long to get to the point. Anti-Detection Writing Skill gives your agent a repeatable editing process: identify what the reader needs, preserve the facts, improve the flow, and review the result. You can also request a measured comparison with GPTZero.


Start with one reply, email, or section of documentation.


1. Install the skill.


npx skills add dexhunter/anti-detection-writing-skill --skill anti-detection-writing


Choose your agent in the installer. The default installation is for the current project.
