How to use Anti-Detection Writing Skill on your next draft

Anti-Detection Writing Skill gives your agent a repeatable editing process: identify what the reader needs, preserve the facts, improve the flow, and review the result. Start with one reply, email, or documentation section. You can separately request a measured GPTZero comparison, with no guarantee of a lower score.

1. Install the skill.

npx skills add dexhunter/anti-detection-writing-skill --skill anti-detection-writing

Choose your agent in the installer. By default, the skill is installed in the current project.

2. Supply the context and original text.

Include your draft, who will read it, and what they need to do. For technical replies, supply the question, relevant documentation, and any version constraints. Then ask:

“Use $anti-detection-writing to make this text smoother and easier to read for [audience]. Preserve facts, code, and qualifications. Remove repetition and irrelevant background. Explain factual corrections and get a separate review of the exact final text.”

Useful edits might move a prerequisite before an instruction, clarify an ambiguous pronoun, or cut a repeated explanation. Check that the advice still means the same thing.

3. Measure a detector result if you need one.

“Compare the original and final text with GPTZero Basic Scan, using the same displayed model. Save exact inputs, all three confidences, dates, warnings, and unchanged or worse results.”

Live scanning requires your own detector access and an agent-supported browser tool; the skill supplies neither. Review correctness and readability separately from the score.
