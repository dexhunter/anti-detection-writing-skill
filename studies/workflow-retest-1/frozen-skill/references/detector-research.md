# Detector research: use and limits

Verified September 6, 2026. Use these findings to assess an editing hypothesis, not to promise a detector outcome. Refresh dated claims when the service changes.

- GPTZero says it stopped using perplexity and burstiness for detection in autumn 2023. Sentence-length variation and unusual vocabulary are not documented controls for its current score. Historical thresholds farther down that support page are not current instructions. [Official support](https://support.gptzero.me/articles/9585228410-how-do-i-interpret-burstiness-or-perplexity)
- Its August 10, 2026 product report says release 4.8b began masking detected headers from the classification features on August 1. That report describes other surrounding material, such as bibliographies, as future work rather than an implemented universal exclusion. It also distinguishes importance-based Advanced highlighting from ordinary sentence classes. This is vendor-reported behavior, not a reproduction of our Basic 4.9b scans. Header edits are therefore a weak new hypothesis; do not infer that changing Markdown or an arbitrary highlighted span will improve a current result. [Product report](https://gptzero.me/news/meaningful-ai-text-detection/)
- Its 2026 report describes supervised document/sentence classification and training against paraphrasing, plus whitespace cleaning and probability remapping. Table 1 identifies version 4.1b; our September 6 UI scans displayed 4.9b. Architecture and hyperparameters remain proprietary. The report does not establish what caused an individual current-service result. A sentence's predicted class and its contribution to a document prediction are different quantities, so a highlight alone is not a causal editing rule. [Report, §§3.2–3.5, §4 and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13042v1)
- DetectGPT, Fast-DetectGPT, and Binoculars evaluate model probabilities through specific algorithms. Their results do not validate a natural-language editing prompt or transfer automatically to another detector. Inspect the actual intervention and tested setting before adapting a paper's lesson. [DetectGPT](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/mitchell23a/mitchell23a.pdf), [Fast-DetectGPT](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.05130v3), [Binoculars](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.12070v3)
- Adversarial Paraphrasing uses detector-guided token generation and evaluates eight open detectors, without GPTZero. Its aggregate detection rates are not individual-document confidences or a current GPTZero pass rate. Copying its prompt omits the implemented mechanism. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07001v2), [authors' implementation](https://github.com/chengez/Adversarial-Paraphrasing/tree/d8515ab705e10b08a1a04214767114522c454bd7)
- Check a paper's quality rubric before borrowing its success criterion. That implementation permits minor factual changes in rating 4, so pooling ratings 4 and 5 does not establish exact claim preservation. Keep material factual failures separate from prose preference; a favorable average rating cannot override changed advice. [Quality rubric](https://github.com/chengez/Adversarial-Paraphrasing/blob/d8515ab705e10b08a1a04214767114522c454bd7/quality_judge_utils.py#L36)

## Additional research checked September 6, 2026

These papers do not identify a GPTZero version matching our Basic 4.9b observations. Read each outcome in its own units: mean class probability, classification rate, and a single document's confidence are different measurements.

| Research | Actual intervention and useful limit |
| --- | --- |
| [Base Models Look Human To AI Detectors / HIP, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19516v1) | Fine-tunes base models on 11,757 paraphrase pairs, then rewrites iteratively. Tests GPTZero and Pangram on 256 passages; reported human probabilities rise while semantic fidelity declines. A high-rated example adds camera details. A [released 0.6B adapter](https://huggingface.co/YixuanEvenXu/Qwen3-0.6B-Base-HIP-adapter/tree/98a907f41440e10aafedd10364b41c32bb5fbf6a) permits local research, but copying its source/target prompt omits the trained mechanism. This does not establish suitability for exact technical instructions. |
| [AuthorMist, March 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.08716v1) | Uses detector-reward training plus selection among eight generated candidates. Its GPTZero attack-success result combines both steps. Embedding similarity and fluency metrics do not verify unchanged numbers or advice. This is not an ordinary rewriting prompt. |
| [Russell et al., ACL 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15654v2) | Generates articles using an expert detection guide and publication examples. GPTZero true-positive rate is lower in a separate humanized batch, while expert readers identify all 30 humanized articles. Different source articles and bundled instructions prevent isolating an editing rule or proving factual preservation. |
| [Why AI Detection Fails for Academic Integrity, August 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.11256v1) | Combines Gemini rewrites with a proprietary humanizer on academic abstracts. High false-negative rates use experimental authorship labels; no claim-level fidelity audit is reported. Longer output and vocabulary changes are correlations, not tested causal instructions. |
| [MASH, January 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08564v1) | Uses trained style embeddings and detector-guided preference optimization, followed by constrained polishing. Tests Writer and Scribbr among other detectors, without GPTZero. Its polishing step starts with already-human-classified outputs; it does not explain how to move a current 100%-AI reply below that threshold. |

When considering a released paraphraser, pin its base model, adapter and inference settings. Check input fit instead of silently truncating. Retain the raw generation separately from the finished candidate: analysis, repeated drafts and incomplete endings must not contaminate the detector input. Independently check exact claims and protected spans before spending scans. A rejection is an unmeasured quality failure, not a zero detector score. A small local feasibility test is not a paper reproduction or evidence that the ordinary skill acquired the model's behavior.

Our September 6 local pilot ran one pass of the released HIP 0.6B adapter on two reused technical answers. Both failed separate factual review: the Kubernetes reply lost a necessary health-condition distinction, and the PostgreSQL reply reversed snapshot timing and added invalid syntax. Original command payloads and URLs survived, illustrating why literal-presence checks alone are insufficient. Neither output was scanned. These failures apply to the two saved outputs, not all HIP sizes or domains; the model was not adopted by the skill.

Distinguish an implemented detector algorithm, a tested editing intervention, and a proposed explanation. Contextual rewriting and an authentic supplied writing sample can be useful editorial choices; a detector benefit remains a separate hypothesis. Apply the experiment and plateau guidance in [measurement.md](measurement.md). Research plausibility alone is not transfer evidence, and these research notes are not a validated score-reduction update.
